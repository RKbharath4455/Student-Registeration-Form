Student Registration Form:

    A Simple and User-friendly Student Registration Form designed to collect and manage student information such as personal details, contact information, academic details, and parent/guardian information.

Project Overview:

    The Student Registration Form provides as easy way for students to enter their required information through a structured registration interface.

Features:

-Student Personal information

-Contact details

-Parent/guardian information

-Academic details

-Address information

-Form Validation

-Simple and user-friendly interface

Form Fields:

-Personal Details which may includes Student Name, DOB, Gender, Blood Group, Aadhaar No.

-Contact Details which may includes Mobile Number, Email Address, Permanent Address, Current Address.

-Parent/Guardian Information which may includes Father Name, Mother Name, Contact Number.

-Academic Details which may includes Degree, Department, Register No, Year of Passing, CGPA/Percentage.

-Then,Finally the Submit and Reset button has been included in it.
